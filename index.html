Type 1

Simple Sentence

One independent clause with a subject and predicate. It expresses one complete thought.

Subject + Verb (+ Object)
  • The dog barked loudly.
  • She runs every morning.
  • Tom and Lisa love pizza.
  • The children played in the park.

Type 2

Compound Sentence

Two independent clauses joined by a coordinating conjunction or semicolon.

Clause + FANBOYS + Clause
  • I was tired, but I kept going.
  • She studied hard, so she passed.
  • He likes tea; she prefers coffee.
  • We ran fast, yet we missed the bus.

FANBOYS Conjunctions

forand norbut oryet so
Type 3

Complex Sentence

One independent clause plus a dependent clause joined by a subordinating conjunction.

Main Clause + Subordinator + Dependent Clause
  • Because it rained, we stayed inside.
  • She left before the show ended.
  • Although he tried, he couldn't win.
  • When I grow up, I want to travel.

Subordinating Conjunctions

becausealthough whenwhile ifsince afterbefore unlessuntil
